# Personal AI Coding Playbook

## 1. When I reach for AI first

I would reach for AI first when I am not familiar with a certain concept or I don't understand what is required or when I need to drill into too much details to uncover insights. I can reach AI when I need to assess several options to make a decision. AI helps by explaining each option, cons and pros, and leave us to decide.

## 2. When I do not reach for AI

I would not reach for AI if a task might involve a privacy or identity leakage or when the subject is senstive and highly confidential. I do not want to expose my personal life style or other peoples private life. Thus, I believe we need to use AI responsibly.

## 3. My non-negotiables

- Privacy: Never expose passwords, API keys, access tokens, credentials, confidential data, or sensitive production information.
- Verification: Never treat AI-generated code, commands, security findings, or technical explanations as correct simply because they look convincing.
- Ownership: If I cannot explain what the AI-generated change does and why it belongs in the project, I should not accept it.

## 4. My review rules

Generated AI content must be verified. It is important to ask for the source of information and also verify the source exits and are legitimite.

## 5. What I am still figuring out

- What type of context should be provided? Is it the same across projects or it is unique. Is there a framework that helps us build or brainstorm what should be provided as context. Same applies for constraints. This is the hardest part I believe, figuring out what constraints should be stated.

| Situation      | AI's best role                         | Your role                              |

| Debugging      | Hypothesis generator                   | Reproduce, test and verify             |
| Infrastructure | Adviser / command explainer            | Understand impact before execution     |
| Testing        | Scenario generator / coverage reviewer | Validate against requirements          |
| Documentation  | Organizer / drafting assistant         | Verify claims against actual evidence  |
